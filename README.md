# 🎮 GuessIt! — The Number Guessing Game

> **One secret number. Limited chances. Can you crack it? 🎯**

GuessIt! is an interactive number guessing game that started as a simple Java console program and was transformed into a fun, responsive web game using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

Choose your difficulty, make your guesses, use hints wisely, and try to beat your best score! 🏆

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Three Difficulty Levels**
  - 🌱 Easy — Numbers from 1–50
  - ⚡ Medium — Numbers from 1–100
  - 🔥 Hard — Numbers from 1–500

- ❤️ **Limited Attempts**
  - Every difficulty gives you a different number of chances.

- 🏆 **Dynamic Scoring**
  - Earn points for guessing correctly.
  - Get bonus points for solving the game early.

- 🔥 **Streak System**
  - Keep winning to increase your streak.

- 💡 **Hint System**
  - Get a hint when you're stuck — but it costs points!

- 📊 **Game Statistics**
  - Track your score, guesses, streak, and attempts.

- 🥇 **Best Score**
  - Your best score is saved in the browser.

- 🎉 **Interactive Feedback**
  - Get different messages depending on whether your guess is too high or too low.

- 📱 **Responsive Design**
  - Play on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Choose a difficulty level.
2. The game generates a secret number.
3. Enter your guess.
4. The game tells you whether your guess is:
   - ⬆️ Too low
   - ⬇️ Too high
   - 🎯 Correct!
5. Use the clues to narrow down the number.
6. Try to guess it before you run out of chances.
7. Beat your previous score! 🏆
